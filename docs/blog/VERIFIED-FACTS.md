## Union wage sheets (examples only, always name the local and the date)

- IBEW Local 340 (Sacramento area) inside wireman sheet effective **August 1, 2026** (https://www.ibewlocal340.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/L340-Inside-Wireman-8-1-2026.pdf), which replaces the June 1, 2026 sheet: journeyman $53.70/hr, total package **$96.77** (the June sheet said $95.77; one fund line rose $1.00); foreman 110% / 115% by crew size; general foreman 125% / 130%; apprentice steps 45–80% ($24.17–$42.96), stepping up at 2,000, 3,500, 5,000 and 6,500 hours, journeyman at 8,000; $60 a day minimum for meals and lodging on travel; a $3.00 raise scheduled for February 1, 2027.
- IBEW Local 11 (Los Angeles) current sheet, **October 26, 2026 to January 31, 2027** (linked from ibew11.org/contracts-wages): journeyman $67.00; foreman 1.15×; general foreman 1.30×; apprentice steps 40% ($26.80) to 85% ($56.95). The earlier January 26 to June 30, 2026 sheet ($65.00; foreman 1.126×, general foreman 1.252×) has expired.
- BLS JOLTS table 4 (https://www.bls.gov/news.release/jolts.t04.htm): 198,000 construction quits in September 2026 (preliminary, 2.4% rate), against 127,000 (1.5%) a year earlier.
- BLS Employee Tenure table 5, January 2026 (https://www.bls.gov/news.release/tenure.t05.htm): median tenure 3.9 years in construction, 4.1 for all workers.
- GSA FY2027 per diem (FTR Bulletin 27-01 on gsa.gov): standard lodging $113 a night, meals and incidentals $68 a day.
- IRS: the moving expense deduction and the exclusion for moving reimbursements are permanently eliminated for most taxpayers (P.L. 119-21, per IRS Publication 15 (2026) and Notice 2026-10); exceptions for active-duty military and, from 2026, intelligence community employees.

## Taxes and law

- IRS: the information-return threshold for Forms 1099-NEC / 1099-MISC rose to **$2,000** for payments made after December 31, 2025, adjusted for inflation from 2027 (IRS Instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC, and https://www.irs.gov/publications/p1099). ECRN's program terms still say "$600 or more": quote them accurately if you quote them, and don't call them wrong; say the IRS threshold changed and readers should check with a tax professional.
- IRS Topic 511 (https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc511): most employees cannot deduct unreimbursed travel expenses; an assignment expected to last over one year is indefinite, not temporary.
- U.S. DOL Wage and Hour (https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets): non-exempt workers get 1.5× pay after 40 hours in a workweek. Davis-Bacon covers federally funded or assisted construction contracts over $2,000 (https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/government-contracts/construction).
- FTC Noncompete Rule: not in effect; court set it aside Aug 20, 2024; FTC dropped its appeal Sep 5, 2025; removed from the CFR effective Feb 12, 2026 (https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/noncompete-rule).
- OSHA Outreach (https://www.osha.gov/training/outreach): the 10-hour and 30-hour classes are voluntary, are not a certification, and may be required by states, cities or employers; the 30-hour suits supervisors.
- OSHA electrical standards page (https://www.osha.gov/electrical/standards): NFPA 70E is a consensus standard, not an OSHA regulation.

## Demand and industry

- U.S. Census, Value of Construction Put in Place, September 2026, released Nov 2, 2026 (https://www.census.gov/construction/c30/pdf/privsa.pdf): private data center construction at a $96.5B seasonally adjusted annual rate, up 90.3% year over year; electric power $146.9B (+14.0%); manufacturing $167.2B (−17.9%); total construction spending −2.7% year over year (https://www.census.gov/construction/c30/pdf/release.pdf).
- LBNL / DOE, United States Data Center Energy Usage Report, 2025 update, June 2026 (https://www.energy.gov/documents/united-states-data-center-energy-usage-report-2025-update): data centers used 4.7% of U.S. electricity in 2024; 11.8% projected for 2030 (range 9.5%–15.3%).
- EIA press release, Sept 9, 2026 (https://www.eia.gov/pressroom/releases/press592.php): record 4,368 billion kWh U.S. generation forecast for 2026, +2.2%.
- DOE Alternative Fuels Data Center (https://afdc.energy.gov/stations/states), as of 11/20/2026: 264,484 non-residential EV charging ports, 80,408 of them DC fast. Counts change daily: say "as of November 2026".
- ABC, Jan 15, 2026 (https://www.abc.org/News-Media/News-Releases/abc-construction-industry-must-attract-349000-workers-in-2026-despite-macroeconomic-headwinds): the industry must attract 349,000 net new workers in 2026 and 456,000 in 2027 (ABC's estimate; attribute it).
- AGC/NCCER 2026 Workforce Survey, Sept 3, 2026 (https://www.agc.org/news/2026/09/03/construction-workforce-shortages-remain-acute-despite-soft-market-conditions-data-centers-strain): 1,830 respondents; 87% have craft openings, 88% of those say as hard or harder to fill; 42% report project delays from worker shortages; 28% did data center work, of whom 58% report more competition for workers and 49% wage pressure; 82% have salaried openings.
- BLS JOLTS table 1 (https://www.bls.gov/news.release/jolts.t01.htm): 298,000 construction job openings in September 2026 (preliminary), against 206,000 a year earlier.
- DOL Office of Apprenticeship construction fact sheet, updated Feb 2026 (https://www.apprenticeship.gov/sites/default/files/DOL_IndFactsheet_Construction-20260319.pdf): 480,399 construction apprentices served in FY2025.
- BEA regional price parities, 2024 (https://www.bea.gov/data/prices-inflation/regional-price-parities-state-and-metro-area): CA 110.7, HI 110.0, NJ 108.8; AR 86.9, MS 87.0, IA 87.8, OK 87.8.
- SHRM 2025 Recruiting Benchmarking Report (https://www.shrm.org/content/dam/en/shrm/research/2025-recruiting-benchmarking-report.pdf): median time to fill about a month and a half, all industries.
