# ECRN content plan (SOP step 12 and Phase 5)

_Written Nov 20, 2026. Review it every quarter, and the first time 2 to 4 weeks after launch, when Search Console has real data._

Three parts:

1. **The page map.** Every public page, what kind of page it is, the search it is built to win, and what links in and out.
2. **The backlog.** The next 16 pages to write, in order, at 2 to 4 a week.
3. **City pages.** When one is allowed, and what Delta has to share first.

## How the research was done, and its limits

- Done on Nov 20, 2026 with live web searches for each topic: what ranks, what kind of site ranks, and what questions the results answer.
- **No search volumes are quoted.** We did not use a keyword tool, and inventing numbers would be worse than having none. Bing Webmaster Tools gives free volumes once the site is verified. Search Console gives real impressions after launch.
- The order of the backlog is a judgment: money pages first, then searches where today's results are weak, then objections, then the employer side.
- Re-rank the backlog by impressions once there is data. A page already showing at positions 8 to 30 is the fastest win.

## 3. City pages

The SOP's rule: **no doorway pages.** A city page needs real local proof: jobs done there, local photos, reviews from there. A page that only swaps the city name gets the whole site marked down.

ECRN has no city pages today, and that is correct.

### What makes a city page allowed

All four, for that metro:

1. **Real open jobs there, week after week.** Not one job once. The board's location data shows which metros qualify.
2. **Real placement numbers from Delta for that metro**, by role, that Delta approves for publication.
3. **A named Delta recruiter who covers that market**, with their consent.
4. **Something only that market has:** BLS pay for that metro area, the local licensing rule from the state or city board, and the project types that are hiring there.

Photos must be real photos from that market, or the page carries none. The site's current photos are generated for ECRN and are illustrative, so they cannot serve as local proof.

## Rules for every new page

From the site contract and SOP Phases 3 and 5.

- Open with a two-sentence answer. Then the detail.
- H2s are the questions people type. Short paragraphs. A table for any comparison.
- Every number, law or code rule has a source the writer opened that day, listed on the page. Prefer primary sources: BLS, IRS, state licensing boards, NECA, IBEW, NFPA, the Census Bureau.
- Say what a number is: the occupation code, the area, the year, median or mean. When BLS has no matching occupation, say which proxy is used.
- 1,100 to 1,700 words. No filler.
- Comparison and objection pages include the real downsides and a "when to skip it" section.
- 4 to 7 FAQs, shown on the page and matched word for word by the FAQPage data.
- Link to the money page, and to at least two other posts or career pages. Never "click here".
- Byline: ECRN Editorial Team, with published and updated dates. Add "Reviewed by" only after a named person at Delta has read it.
- No testimonials, counts, client names, awards or license claims that Delta has not given in writing.
- No legal, tax or financial advice. State the rule, cite it, and tell the reader to check with a professional.
- The same day it ships: add the 3 links in, request indexing in Search Console, and let IndexNow send it.
