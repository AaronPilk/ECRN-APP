## The rules

- **You create every account and type every password, code and CAPTCHA.** Claude never does.
- **Only facts we have.** No address, no phone number, no hours, no founder name, no reviews, no member counts. If a form demands one of these, stop and ask.
- **Same text everywhere, character for character.** AI engines and Google compare the profiles. Copy from this file, do not retype.
- Claude submits to a directory only after you OK a named batch. 10 to 15 a week, not all at once.
- Skip anything that charges, that posts publicly on your behalf, or that asks AI agents not to submit. Paid memberships are your decision.
- "Free if you add our badge" is a link swap. You decide each one.
- Do not say the app is "in the App Store" or "on Google Play" until it is. Say: on the web, with iPhone and Android apps.

### Where each profile URL goes in the code

One place: `BRAND.sameAs` in `lib/site.ts`. Claude adds each URL the day you send it.

```ts
/** Add social/LinkedIn/Crunchbase profile URLs here as they're claimed. */
sameAs: [
  "https://www.linkedin.com/company/<the page>",
  "https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/<the profile>",
  "https://x.com/<handle>",
  "https://www.instagram.com/<handle>/",
  "https://www.facebook.com/<page>",
  "https://www.youtube.com/@<handle>",
] as string[],
```

That list becomes the `sameAs` line of the Organization data on every page. It is how search engines and AI engines learn that those profiles and ecrn.com are the same organization. The site already lists Delta's LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook under the parent company. Those three were checked against Delta's own site on Nov 20, 2026 and match.

## 5. Local listings (Phase 4): not for ECRN

**ECRN has no walk-in location and no in-person customers, so it does not get local listings.**

- The SOP says to skip Phase 4 for online-only sites, and that Google penalizes fake addresses and virtual offices.
- Google's own eligibility rules exclude online-only brands from Business Profiles.
- Listing ECRN at Delta's address would put two listings on one address for the same service. That can put Delta's own listing at risk.

So Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business, Yelp, BBB and Nextdoor all belong to **Delta Construction Partners**, and whether and how to run them is your call with Delta.

What Delta can do that helps ECRN, without bending any rule:

1. Link to ecrn.com from Delta's website.
2. Mention ECRN in Delta's Google Business Profile posts, with a link, if Delta has a profile.
3. Add ECRN to the "About" text on Delta's LinkedIn page, and add ECRN as an affiliated page.

No address, phone number or opening hours for ECRN should ever be written down anywhere. There are none.
